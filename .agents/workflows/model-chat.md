# Workflow: Model Chat Debate
When tasked with a complex design or architectural decision, use this workflow to simulate a rigorous multi-perspective debate.

1. **Define Personas:** Establish three distinct technical personas:
    - **The Systems Thinker:** Focuses on scalability, broad architecture, and long-term maintainability.
    - **The Pragmatist:** Focuses on shipping fast, reducing complexity, and keeping dependencies absolutely minimal.
    - **The Security Auditor (Contrarian):** Looks for flaws, edge cases, attack vectors, and potential catastrophic failures.
2. **Simulate Debate:** Write out a detailed (but concise) internal conversation where these three personas discuss the user's problem. Each persona must present their unique approach and rigorously critique the others.
3. **Converge:** After the debate, synthesize the strongest points from all three personas into a single, unified, battle-tested architecture.
4. **Present Results:** Output the synthesized architectural plan to the user clearly, highlighting why this converged solution is superior to a naive first-guess approach.
